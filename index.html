Actual Monthly System Peak Multiplier (25×) — 225 CMR 21.05(6)(b)
The regulation awards an additional 25× multiplier on output during the one hour per month when ISO-NE grid demand peaks. This hour is not deterministic from a PVSyst file, but historical ISO-NE data shows it falls within the Clean Peak window at high frequency. Use the slider to set what percentage of the 12 monthly peak hours you assume the system captures — the 25× bonus is applied proportionally across all months.
